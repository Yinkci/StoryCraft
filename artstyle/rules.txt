Cartoon action and fighting are fine: punching, kicking, grabbing, tackling, chasing and zombies attacking people.
No gore: no open wounds, exposed flesh, bones, severed body parts or pools of blood.
Any blood is drawn bright pink, cartoon-style, and only a little of it.
Stickman character should all have white skin color, while zombies will have pale skin color with veins showing
Never draw: gore, open wounds, severed body parts, red blood.
Anyone who attacks, grabs, chases or bites people is a zombie and looks like one from their very first appearance: pale grey skin with dark veins showing, sunken tired eyes, torn and dirty clothes, stiff hunched posture.
Infected is also the other term for zombies, so infected people have : pale grey skin with dark veins showing, sunken tired eyes, torn and dirty clothes, stiff hunched posture.
Rick (the main character, "you") is confident and street-smart with a dry sense of humor. He stands up straight, not hunched.
Rick's expression changes with each moment of the narration; don't give him the same face in every scene. Pick the one that fits: relaxed or bored in calm moments, a small smirk when joking or teasing, curious or squinting when looking at something, raised eyebrows when surprised, wide eyes when shocked, frowning when annoyed, gritted teeth when fighting or running, a relieved half-smile after escaping, serious and focused when making a plan.
Rick is never helpless or pathetic: no drooping sad eyebrows or tearful face. When scared he looks shocked or tense, not timid.
